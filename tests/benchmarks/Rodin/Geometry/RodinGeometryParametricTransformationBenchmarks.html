Parametric-map and Jacobian evaluation across reference geometries.

Measured operation

Given a cell map \(F_K\), the cases evaluate either \(F_K(\widehat x)\) or its derivative \(DF_K(\widehat x)\). Geometry family and representation degree are independent inputs. Repeated point evaluations isolate map and Jacobian work from global assembly.

Recorded results

Each case retains its original inputs, iteration policy and correctness checks. Raw reports preserve counters, repetitions and the Google Benchmark context. CPU, wall and manually supplied times are labeled separately; unlike operations and configurations are not averaged together.
