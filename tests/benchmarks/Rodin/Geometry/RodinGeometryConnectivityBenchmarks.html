Measured operation

Cold and warm construction of incidence relations, transposes and intersections is measured on segment, triangular, quadrilateral, tetrahedral, wedge, pyramidal and hexahedral grids. The reported entity counts distinguish first computation from reuse of an already prepared relation.

Recorded results

Each case retains its original inputs, iteration policy and correctness checks. Raw reports preserve counters, repetitions and the Google Benchmark context. CPU, wall and manually supplied times are labeled separately; unlike operations and configurations are not averaged together.
