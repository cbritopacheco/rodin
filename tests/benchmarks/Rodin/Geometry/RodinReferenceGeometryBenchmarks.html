Paired reference-table and pointwise geometry Jacobian measurements.

Measured operation

Measures bulk Jacobians against the original pointwise fallback.

Both paths evaluate \(J_{ji}(\widehat x_q)= \sum_a X_{ja}\partial_i\phi_a(\widehat x_q)\) with the same vertex basis and accumulation order. The pointwise measurement explicitly calls the base transformation's bulk fallback; no second production path is added. Setup, warmup and exact entry comparisons are outside timing. Repeated calls retain output-vector capacity. Physical Jacobians are timed, but no physical-coordinate evaluation, FE assembly or solve is timed. Results are local diagnostics, not CTest or CI gates.

Recorded results

Each case retains its original inputs, iteration policy and correctness checks. Raw reports preserve counters, repetitions and the Google Benchmark context. CPU, wall and manually supplied times are labeled separately; unlike operations and configurations are not averaged together.
