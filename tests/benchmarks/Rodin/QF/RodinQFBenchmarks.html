Reference quadrature-rule access and integration.

Measured operation

The quadrature families provide samples \(\{(\widehat x_q,w_q)\}\) for reference-cell integration, \(Q(f)=\sum_q w_q f(\widehat x_q)\). Cases exercise rule construction and integration across the registered reference geometries and orders; their names distinguish these scopes.

Recorded results

Each case retains its original inputs, iteration policy and correctness checks. Raw reports preserve counters, repetitions and the Google Benchmark context. CPU, wall and manually supplied times are labeled separately; unlike operations and configurations are not averaged together.
