PETSc local/MPI warmed physics assembly with aligned iterations.

Measured operation

Complete warmed assembly measured independently of setup and oracles. Architecture: identical forms are configured through PhysicsForm. MPI meshes use explicit incidence and root partitioning; only owned cells contribute to workload counts. Fixed iteration counts align collectives. Assembly is timed between steady-clock readings; MPI reports maximum rank duration. Synchronization, timing reduction, and numerical checks are outside timing.

Recorded results

Each case retains its original inputs, iteration policy and correctness checks. Raw reports preserve counters, repetitions and the Google Benchmark context. CPU, wall and manually supplied times are labeled separately; unlike operations and configurations are not averaged together.
