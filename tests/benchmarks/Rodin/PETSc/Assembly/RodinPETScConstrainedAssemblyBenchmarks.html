Full constrained PETSc local/MPI assembly, with aligned timing.

Measured operation

Measures complete PETSc problem assembly with independent patch checks. Fixed iterations align collective calls. The maximum rank duration includes constraint assembly and matrix/vector completion but excludes the preceding barrier, timing reduction, interpolation and numerical checks.

Recorded results

Each case retains its original inputs, iteration policy and correctness checks. Raw reports preserve counters, repetitions and the Google Benchmark context. CPU, wall and manually supplied times are labeled separately; unlike operations and configurations are not averaged together.
