Expression binding with prepared quadrature compared with original loops.

Measured operation

Paired cases evaluate equivalent expression trees using retained quadrature binding or the original entry loop. The registered input and binding variants distinguish rule preparation from repeated evaluation. Both sides retain the same physical operation, so the comparison measures expression reuse rather than a changed quadrature formula.

Recorded results

Each case retains its original inputs, iteration policy and correctness checks. Raw reports preserve counters, repetitions and the Google Benchmark context. CPU, wall and manually supplied times are labeled separately; unlike operations and configurations are not averaged together.
