Specialized rank-one Jacobian assembly compared with generic dispatch.

Measured operation

The form contracts coefficient-weighted trial and test Jacobians. Identity coefficients with identical types select the specialized quadrature rule; a numerically identical coefficient with a distinct type selects the generic rule. Both routes assemble the same operator, separating dispatch and contraction costs from the mathematical problem. Mesh, space and form construction precede timing; repeated assembly is included.

Recorded results

Each case retains its original inputs, iteration policy and correctness checks. Raw reports preserve counters, repetitions and the Google Benchmark context. CPU, wall and manually supplied times are labeled separately; unlike operations and configurations are not averaged together.
