Architecture

Each scope binds one of the four distinct operator types in the coupled reaction-diffusion workload. It includes formula selection, owned mapped quadrature, basis preparation and local matrix contraction; it excludes global insertion, constraints and solving. The mesh, element degrees and order match the Assembly-owned consumer. Reference-table first use and numerical checks are outside timing. Mesh traversal remains included.

Every cell matrix is qualified against the existing original entry-loop oracle, which shares mapped geometry but not production basis reuse. A perturbed entry must fail that comparison. A separate untimed sweep after measurement must reproduce every initially retained entry exactly.

Recorded results

Each case retains its original inputs, iteration policy and correctness checks. Raw reports preserve counters, repetitions and the Google Benchmark context. CPU, wall and manually supplied times are labeled separately; unlike operations and configurations are not averaged together.
