Measured operation

Measures complete assembly against a frozen full-matrix oracle.

Contexts are scalar mass, diffusion, Helmholtz, vector mass and linear elasticity, with constant or represented field coefficients. First includes form construction, assembly and destruction; Warm retains the form. Ordinary scopes retain mapped geometry. ColdGeometry flushes the mesh's transformation and mapped-quadrature caches before each assembly, outside timing. It includes lazy geometry reconstruction, not merely index-slot allocation, and does not alter topology, spaces or coefficients. These controls must not be interpreted as pure cache allocation latencies. PauseOnly uses identical timer boundaries without changing mesh state, providing a negative control for the measurement protocol. Every final sparse operator is compared with the same precomputed Integral oracle. No solve or error integration is timed. Profilers are opt-in and are not CTest or CI timing gates.

Recorded results
