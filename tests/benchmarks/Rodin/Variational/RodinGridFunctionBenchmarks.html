Interpolation and re-interpolation benchmarks with numerical oracles.

Measured operation

Meshes, spaces, analytic functions and quadrature points are prepared before timing. Each operation checks the final value against the analytic field. Tabulated evaluation repeats one sample or alternates samples in the same cell. Cross-space assignment uses distinct spaces on the same mesh and therefore interpolates the source rather than copying its coefficients.

Recorded results

Each case retains its original inputs, iteration policy and correctness checks. Raw reports preserve counters, repetitions and the Google Benchmark context. CPU, wall and manually supplied times are labeled separately; unlike operations and configurations are not averaged together.
