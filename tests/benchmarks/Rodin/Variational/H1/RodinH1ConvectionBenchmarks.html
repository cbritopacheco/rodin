Measures H1 directional-gradient preparation and local contraction.

Measured operation

Mathematical foundation

The measured form is \(a_b(u,v)=\int_K(Ju)b\cdot\overline v\,dx\). Fields are real or complex; complex fields admit either real or genuinely complex directions. Real fields use real directions, as required by the current shape-Dot range constraint. An independent expression-entry quadrature loop and a deliberate perturbation qualify every matrix before timing. The final matrix must repeat exactly. A defective baseline is not timed for the unsupported coefficient semantics.

Architecture

Binding includes owned mapped quadrature, scalar tabulation lookup, physical gradients and local contraction. Traversal, insertion and solving are absent. Warm scopes reuse one order; Refresh alternates two orders on the same owner. Degree, components, quadrature samples and both physical dimensions are reported separately. This component executable is opt-in and outside CTest.

Recorded results

Each case retains its original inputs, iteration policy and correctness checks. Raw reports preserve counters, repetitions and the Google Benchmark context. CPU, wall and manually supplied times are labeled separately; unlike operations and configurations are not averaged together.
