Finite-element integrator kernels and their assembly consumers.

Measured operation

Cases compare local mass and diffusion integrator entry evaluation with the corresponding global assembly operations. Trial and test spaces, geometry and polynomial order identify each workload. Kernel preparation and global scatter are distinct scopes in the registered case names; timings from those scopes should be compared separately.

Recorded results

Each case retains its original inputs, iteration policy and correctness checks. Raw reports preserve counters, repetitions and the Google Benchmark context. CPU, wall and manually supplied times are labeled separately; unlike operations and configurations are not averaged together.
