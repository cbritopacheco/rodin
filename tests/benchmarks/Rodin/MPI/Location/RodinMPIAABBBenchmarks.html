Measured operation

Paired workloads cover the reference-cell families through local search, a restricted candidate subset and MPI location. Rank counts remain part of the configuration. The drivers qualify returned membership and point data before timing, separating communication and partition effects from geometric correctness.

Recorded results

Each case retains its original inputs, iteration policy and correctness checks. Raw reports preserve counters, repetitions and the Google Benchmark context. CPU, wall and manually supplied times are labeled separately; unlike operations and configurations are not averaged together.
