Warmed constrained native assembly with manufactured numerical checks.

Measured operation

The public assembly operation is repeated on a fixed sparse layout. Manufactured solutions and independent reference calculations qualify the matrix and load before timing; repeated-call checks detect altered results. Constraint processing belongs to the measured assembly operation, while fixture preparation and reference construction do not.

Recorded results

Each case retains its original inputs, iteration policy and correctness checks. Raw reports preserve counters, repetitions and the Google Benchmark context. CPU, wall and manually supplied times are labeled separately; unlike operations and configurations are not averaged together.
