Poisson assembly benchmark coverage

Measured operation

Benchmarks assembly of a scalar P1 Poisson problem on a 16x16 triangular grid, with both unit diffusion and explicit constant coefficient forms. The cases isolate form assembly cost from mesh and finite-element-space setup.

Recorded results

Each case retains its original inputs, iteration policy and correctness checks. Raw reports preserve counters, repetitions and the Google Benchmark context. CPU, wall and manually supplied times are labeled separately; unlike operations and configurations are not averaged together.
