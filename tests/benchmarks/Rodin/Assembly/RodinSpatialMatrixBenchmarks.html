Matrix-valued global assembly compared with nine scalar blocks.

Measured operation

A three-by-three matrix form is assembled directly and as nine scalar forms. The benchmark checks both sparse and triplet assembly against the scalar-block reference before recording repeated assembly. This measures the whole assembly consumer of spatial-matrix arithmetic, including global insertion, rather than the cost of a single matrix product.

Recorded results

Each case retains its original inputs, iteration policy and correctness checks. Raw reports preserve counters, repetitions and the Google Benchmark context. CPU, wall and manually supplied times are labeled separately; unlike operations and configurations are not averaged together.
