Measures the StokesAssembly workload through its public library pipeline.

Measured operation

Measures repeated public Stokes assembly, including constraints and global pressure-mean completion. Setup checks the exact affine patch residual and rejects wrong-field and missing-pressure controls without factorizing the system. Every timed assembly is checked against the exact field outside timing.

Recorded results

Each case retains its mesh, finite element spaces, quadrature and iteration policy. Solver-specific coefficient and solve checks belong to the solver benchmark. Raw reports preserve counters, repetitions and the Google Benchmark context. CPU, wall and manually supplied times are labeled separately; unlike operations and configurations are not averaged together.
