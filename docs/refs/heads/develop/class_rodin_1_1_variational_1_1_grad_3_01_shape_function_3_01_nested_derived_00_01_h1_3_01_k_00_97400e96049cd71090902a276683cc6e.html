Each explicit bind prepares the physical gradients from the supplied point's inverse Jacobian. Cell and quadrature indices do not identify geometry state: a deformation can preserve all of them. Storage is retained for subsequent basis access, while reference tabulation keeps its existing formula-identity lifetime contract.
