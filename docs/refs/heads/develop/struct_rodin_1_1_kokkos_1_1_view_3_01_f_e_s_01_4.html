Architecture

The view retains the space's prepared device DOF handles and its physical value dimension. Scalar reference bases are replicated by component, preserving the P1, H1 and P0g numbering and sparse component optimizations. An intrinsic vector basis has one coefficient per basis function, independently of its physical value dimension. Its DOF view supplies physical basis values and the supported differential operators, including any Piola mapping and cell orientation transformations. No nodal interpolation is assumed here.

getCount() and getGlobalIndex() describe complete local basis functions. getComponentCount(), getStride() and getCapacity() retain the grouped representation used by existing assembly kernels; intrinsic vector bases have one component group and zero component stride. All data are prepared by the space before constructing this view; evaluation is const.
