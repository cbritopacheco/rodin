Architecture

Native first-order maps use their vertex hull without allocation. Higher orders convert a certified reference-space representation into a positive partition of unity, including the rational collapsed pyramid basis. Reference conversions are shared; physical coefficients are owned. Conversion roundoff is included in every support query. An unreliable conversion gives infinite support, never a sampled membership filter.

Bounds(const PolytopeTransformation& transformation, Polytope::Type geometry, size_t degree)

Constructs the enclosure of a certified reference-space map.

Parameters
transformation Coordinate map to enclose.
geometry Reference geometry of that map.
degree Certified degree of the containing control space.

The caller must establish containment in the degree-p Bernstein reference space: simplex, tensor, triangle-segment product, or rational collapsed pyramid. This is a representation bound, not a family index inferred from an arbitrary transformation's samples.
