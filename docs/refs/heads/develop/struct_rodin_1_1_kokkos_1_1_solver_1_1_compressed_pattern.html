Compressed pattern of a square matrix, without values.

Architecture

Transpose retains the input positions of the transposed entries. Cholesky constructs an elimination tree and its lower-factor pattern. LU propagates symbolic row reach through previously eliminated columns. The constructors operate on valid compressed arrays and permutations on the host; no numeric values or device allocation participate.

Computes the CSR pattern of the Cholesky factor of .

Parameters
n Order of the matrix.
starts Line starts of the compressed arrays of or .
indices Indices of the compressed arrays.
q Original index of every position of the ordering.
Returns Pattern of the lower triangular factor, rows sorted.

The elimination tree is built first, and the pattern of every row is then the set of nodes reached by climbing the tree from the entries of the row below the diagonal. The cost is proportional to the number of entries of the factor. The diagonal closes every row.

Parameters

p Original row of every pivot row.

Returns Pattern of the bundled factors, rows sorted.

Every column of the factors is the set of rows reached in the graph of the previous columns of from the entries of the column of , as in the left-looking algorithm of Gilbert and Peierls. The cost is that of the reachability searches, bounded by the number of operations of the factorization. The diagonal is always kept.
