static size_t Rodin::Adaptation::SWIFT::Parameters::Quadrature::getCellOrder(size_t feOrder, size_t transformationOrder = 1, bool simplex = true)

Calibrated P1 or provisional higher-order volume integration policy.

Parameters
feOrder Polynomial order of the displacement finite element.
transformationOrder Polynomial order of the geometry transformation.
simplex Whether the entity is a simplex; tensor-product degree one is not P1.
Returns Two for affine P1; otherwise at least eight. Not an exactness guarantee.
