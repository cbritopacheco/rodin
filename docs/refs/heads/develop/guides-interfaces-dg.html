Facet contributions

Cell integrals describe volume contributions. Facet integrals express exterior fluxes and coupling between neighboring cells. The integration domain, one-sided values and normal orientation must be specified together.

Select the facet domain

FormDomainUse
BoundaryIntegralExterior facetsNeumann, Robin and weak Dirichlet terms
InterfaceIntegralInterior facetsMaterial interfaces and DG coupling
FaceIntegralAll facetsTerms common to both domains

.over(attribute, ...) restricts a form to tagged facets. Prepare the facet-to-cell relation first. BoundaryNormal provides the exterior normal; FaceNormal follows the library's facet orientation.

Choose a side

A conforming scalar field has equal values on the two sides of an interior facet. A discontinuous field or a conforming field's gradient can have distinct traces. For derivative expressions, Grad(field).traceOf(attribute) selects the incident cell with that region label. Ambiguous derivative evaluation raises UndeterminedTraceDomainException. For a discontinuous field value, evaluate on a point mapped into the selected cell, as in the checked example.

mesh.trace({{attr1, attr2}, interfaceAttribute}) assigns a facet label from a pair of cell labels. This operation prepares attribute selection for subsequent interface forms; the selected field trace still specifies its incident region.

The analysis of interior-penalty formulations is developed in [2]. For suitable discontinuous spaces, regular meshes and a sufficiently large penalty, the symmetric Dirichlet diffusion form is coercive and yields an SPD matrix. Solver choice follows the assembled form and constraints. Conforming P1 fields have zero interior value jumps, while P0 has zero cellwise gradient. A DG discretization therefore requires a space and facet operators appropriate to the full chosen formulation.
