Rodin/Heart/CCMLC2014/Physics/PassiveStress.h file

Passive and viscous stress evaluators for the CCMLC2014 reduced 0D dynamics.

Contents

  • Reference
    • Namespaces
    • Classes

Provides the PassiveStressEvaluator and ViscousStressEvaluator classes. The passive stress is derived from the reduced strain-energy density via chain rule through the reduced invariants (see Caruel et al. 2014, §2.2 and §4). The viscous stress models viscoelastic damping in the thin-wall shell.
