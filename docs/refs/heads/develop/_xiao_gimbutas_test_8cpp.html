Function documentation

TEST(XiaoGimbutasTest, ShippedRulesAreNotFullySymmetric)

The shipped rules are not fully symmetric, and that is the point.

Node elimination buys a smaller rule by giving up invariance under the element's symmetry group, which is the trade this family makes against Rodin::QF::WitherdenVincent. Asserting the asymmetry keeps the two families honestly distinguished, and confirms that the symmetry check used on the symmetric family can in fact fail.

TEST(XiaoGimbutasTest, TabulatedCountsMeetThePublishedOnes)

Every tabulated rule has the published point count.

These rules are measured against the Xiao–Gimbutas counts rather than the symmetric Witherden–Vincent counts. The two families publish different numbers for the same element and cannot be compared interchangeably.
