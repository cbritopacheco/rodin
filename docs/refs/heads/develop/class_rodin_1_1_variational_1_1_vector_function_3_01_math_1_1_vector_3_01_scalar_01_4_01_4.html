Retains a temporary dense vector for the lifetime of its expression copies.

Parameters
v Dense vector whose storage is transferred to the function.

Lvalue construction continues to borrow its vector. Rvalue construction shares immutable owned storage across expression copies; evaluation uses the same direct reference in both cases.
