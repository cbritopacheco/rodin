Each basis Jacobian has one row per field component and one column per ambient coordinate. Reference gradients have entries, while their physical images under have entries, including on embedded cells and zero-dimensional points.

Every explicit bind prepares physical derivatives from the supplied point. Cell and formula identities do not identify geometry state: deformation may preserve both while changing the inverse Jacobian. Only reference tabulation and working storage are reused across binds.
