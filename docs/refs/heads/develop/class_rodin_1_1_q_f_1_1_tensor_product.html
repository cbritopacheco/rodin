TensorProduct final

Cartesian product of two quadrature formulae.

Defined in Rodin/QF/TensorProduct.h

class TensorProduct final;

Contents

  • Reference
    • Inherits from
    • Constructors, destructors, conversion operators
    • Public functions

The coordinates of the right factor are appended to those of the left factor and the corresponding weights are multiplied. This covers both tensor-product cells and products of unlike factors, such as a triangular rule times a segment rule on a wedge.
