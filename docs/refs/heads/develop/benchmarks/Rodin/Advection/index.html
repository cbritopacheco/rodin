Advection

Published configurations

Transport and advection of fields.

Lagrangian advances scalar fields on unstructured meshes by tracing characteristics and projecting the transported field into a finite element space. It is used to transport level sets and other evolving fields; boundary policies control the treatment of characteristics that reach the mesh boundary.
