Solid

Published configurations

Constitutive laws, kinematics, and solid-mechanics integration.

KinematicState computes deformation measures for constitutive laws such as NeoHookean and HolzapfelOgden. InternalVirtualWork assembles residuals and consistent tangents, and stress fields such as CauchyStress support postprocessing. The module covers finite-strain elasticity and fiber-reinforced material models.
