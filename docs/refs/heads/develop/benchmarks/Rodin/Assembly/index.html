Assembly

Published configurations

Global assembly of discrete operators.

Sequential evaluates local integrals and accumulates global matrices and vectors; Default selects the configured assembly strategy. BilinearFormAssemblyInput supplies spaces and integrators, and ConstraintMap expands degree-of-freedom constraints when assembling constrained or coupled problems.
