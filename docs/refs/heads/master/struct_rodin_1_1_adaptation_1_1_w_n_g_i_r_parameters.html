bool Rodin::Adaptation::WNGIRParameters::rigidDiagnostics

Compute the rigid-observation coercivity diagnostics.

The initial and final rigid-mode states are reported but never read by the solve, and they cost a generalized eigenproblem each. Set to false to skip them when the report fields are not needed.
