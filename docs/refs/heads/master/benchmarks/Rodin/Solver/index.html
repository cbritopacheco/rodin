Solver

Published configurations

Solution of linear and nonlinear discrete systems.

CG handles symmetric positive-definite systems, GMRES handles general iterative solves, and SparseLU provides direct sparse factorization. NewtonSolver drives nonlinear solves. These solvers operate on prepared systems or variational problems and provide convergence controls for PDE and optimization calculations.
