Density optimization examples

Density optimization varies a material coefficient on a fixed domain. A state solve determines temperature, an adjoint supplies sensitivity, and a regularized update changes the density within its admissible interval.

TutorialMain idea
Temperature minimizationSIMP conductivity, adjoint sensitivity and a projected material update

Begin with the Poisson solve. The thermal tutorial supplies computed density and temperature fields, objective and material histories, and the complete source. Its fixed iteration budget defines the recorded experiment; stationarity or a prescribed material budget requires an associated optimization criterion. The density optimization overview develops the state–sensitivity–update sequence.

Shape optimization examples

Shape optimization varies the material boundary of an elastic body. Compliance and an area penalty define the objective; a regularized shape derivative supplies the geometry update. Both tutorials use MMG and require a hydrated input mesh from the repository's Git LFS resources.

TutorialMain idea
Shape optimization of a cantileverExplicit boundary displacement followed by remeshing
Optimizing a level-set cantileverSigned-field transport and body-fitted interface reconstruction

Start with the boundary-deformation example after the elasticity tutorial. Compare compliance, area and objective at solved states, then inspect Jacobian signs and element quality after each update. The level-set tutorial records both increasing objective steps and a reproduced low-quality triangle, making step acceptance and geometric quality part of interpreting its fixed-budget run. The shape optimization overview explains the two representations and prerequisites.
