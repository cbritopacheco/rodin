WitnessLattice final

Cached generating lattice for reference coverings and offline comparisons.

Simplex points have barycentric coordinates in integer multiples of . Tensor cells use Cartesian grids, wedges use triangular grids times segments, and pyramids use shrinking square layers. All vertices are included without supplemental points. Weights sum to reference volume. SWIFT samples QualityCovering instead; this lattice supplies its geometry-specific indices and supports offline covering comparisons. The quadrature interface supplies mapped-point caching, not polynomial exactness or a continuous quality certificate.
