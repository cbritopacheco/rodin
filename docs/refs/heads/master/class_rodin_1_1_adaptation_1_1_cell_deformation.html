Rodin::Adaptation::CellDeformation class

Lazily evaluated deformation state of a mesh cell.

Contents

  • Reference
    • Constructors, destructors, conversion operators
    • Public functions

Given the displacement gradient at a point, this holds the deformation gradient and derives, on demand, the quantities mesh adaptation needs: the Jacobian , the inverse transpose , the relative distortion

and its gradient . Each is computed at most once per state and cached, following the lazy mutable Rodin::Optional idiom of Rodin::Geometry::Point.

This is the mesh-adaptation counterpart of Rodin::Solid::Kinematics::KinematicState. It differs deliberately in two ways, both required here:

  • it is lazy: an admissibility sweep needs only at most points, and never needs , or , so computing them eagerly would be wasted work at every validation point;
  • it does not assume . Mesh adaptation must evaluate inverted and degenerate states precisely in order to detect and reject them, so admissibility is reported through isAdmissible rather than asserted. Quantities that are undefined for (getInverseTranspose, getRelativeDistortion, getRelativeDistortionGradient) must only be requested once isAdmissible holds.
