Parameters

Copies source mesh to a destination mesh.

Parameters
src in Source MMG mesh.
dst out Destination MMG mesh.

Performs deep-copy allocation for dynamically owned MMG arrays. Cached cell adjacencies use MMG's three entries per triangle or four entries per tetrahedron, with five additional array entries.
