QuadratureFormulaBase

Read-only access to reference quadrature points and weights.

Defined in Rodin/QF/QuadratureFormula.h

class QuadratureFormulaBase;

Concrete families own their samples. Immediate numerical operations may borrow this interface while its owner remains alive and unchanged. Persistent consumers require QuadratureFormula instead.
