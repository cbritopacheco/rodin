This catalogue presents Rodin's performance benchmarks, from individual mathematical operations to assembled finite element problems. Each benchmark describes the operation measured, the inputs and the measurement conditions. Published timing histories help track changes across commits for a selected configuration and benchmark case.

Benchmarks are organized by their owning module, following the library's directory hierarchy. The module groups below provide entry points to classes and workloads; MPI and PETSc benchmarks remain within their respective modules. Physical contexts, such as Stokes, collect benchmarks whose meaning depends on a mathematical model. Descriptions become more detailed as the navigation reaches individual benchmarks, explaining what is measured and why. Use the search to find a class, operation or workload directly. Modules without registered benchmarks are identified on their pages.
